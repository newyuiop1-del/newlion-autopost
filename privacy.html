Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

NewLion Auto Post ("the App") is a desktop tool that helps a creator publish their own short videos to their own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App uses and how it is handled.

1. Data we access from TikTok

When you choose "Log in with TikTok", you authorize the App with these scopes:

  • user.info.basic — your display name and avatar, shown in the App so you can confirm which account you are posting to.
  • video.upload and video.publish — to upload a video you selected and publish it to your TikTok account with the caption and privacy setting you chose.

The App also reads TikTok's creator info (allowed privacy levels, maximum video length, and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are available) so it only offers options your account supports.

2. Where data is stored

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored only on your own computer, in a file readable only by your user account. They are never sent to us or to any server other than TikTok.
  • Your videos, captions and cover images stay on your computer until you choose to post them. They are sent only to TikTok's official API.
  • The App keeps a local log of what was posted (video file name, time, post status and post link) so the same video is not posted twice.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not run servers that collect your data. There is no account with us.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not read your followers, messages, comments or other private TikTok data.

4. Your choices

  • You can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • You can delete all stored tokens and logs by deleting the App's folder from your computer.

5. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
